Angela Ding

Data engineer with 10 years of experience building distributed systems and large-scale batch and stream processing pipelines. Formerly a software engineer at Google Search. AWS-certified, Chicago-based.

About

I build the systems that move data between systems and am passionate about building tools that help engineers move faster. Over the past decade I've worked across the stack of large-scale data, from real-time market data feeds on AWS to the infrastructure behind Google Search.

I started at CME Group, where I migrated the market data pipeline to AWS, work that resulted in the decommission of a 100-node on-premise Hadoop cluster, and built a real-time Flink application loading roughly 500 million messages a day. At Capital One, I worked on a horizontal data platform supporting more than 10,000 business-critical metrics.

Most recently, as a software engineer in Google Search, I contributed to efforts that reduced Search latency, redesigned APIs for shared UI components, and built developer productivity features used daily by Search engineers.

I studied Computer Science and Statistics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.
